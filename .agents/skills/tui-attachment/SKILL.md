# TUI attachment

## Permanent boundaries

- Attach to an existing daemon; never spawn, kill, or simulate authoritative game state. Submit graceful shutdown and new-playthrough requests only through their explicit typed daemon operations.
- Render authoritative snapshots and send typed actions through one connection worker responsible for handshake, subscriptions, requests, unsubscribe, and reconnect.
- Limit local state to view focus, selections, onboarding alignment proposals, contextual UI state, bounded received caches, observed delivery rate, scroll state, help, and stale age.
- Never read daemon logs or reconstruct hidden resource, mission, world, research, or story values in widgets.
- Retry failures every second, retain the latest snapshot with an obvious stale indicator, make immediate retry available, and distinguish detach-without-shutdown from the session menu's explicit save-and-stop request.
- Render from pure application state and restore raw mode, cursor, and screen on every exit, error, and panic path.

## Current and target interfaces

- Open a session menu on launch and with `Q`, except skip it automatically on a fresh world (`!onboarding.complete`) so new players enter straight into Awakening -> Coherence, and honor `--resume` / `SUBSTRATE_RESUME` for immediate returning reattach. Keep Continue/Begin, Detach, Save & Stop, and New Playthrough semantically distinct: Detach leaves simulation active; Save & Stop waits for daemon acknowledgement before restoring the terminal; New Playthrough requires a second confirmation and reports persistence rollback clearly. Never delete or rewrite save files in the TUI.
- Run the event-driven input/render loop (`crossterm` `EventStream` + `tokio::select!`) so keypresses redraw immediately, active motion renders at 30 FPS (`33 ms`), waveforms at 15 FPS, ambient beacons/blueprints at 4 FPS (`250-350 ms`), and static views sleep with zero redraws. NEVER call `now_or_never()` on `EventStream::next()` (crossterm 0.29 leaves a no-op waker that stalls input until the next timer tick); handle one stream event per `select!` iteration.
- Keep the skippable Boot Intro (`boot.rs`) strictly operational (no unapproved lore): ~2.6s system initialization with planetary limb rise (`cinematic.rs`) on fresh/pending onboarding or New Playthrough, ~0.75s power-on wipe on returning attaches (letting navigation keys fall through on the same press), skipped immediately on any key, and bypassed on `TERM=dumb`. Keep 180 ms CRT horizontal retrace wipes (`art::render_crt_wipe`) interruptible on any keypress and bypassed on `TERM=dumb`.
- Keep Coherence onboarding full-bleed, graphical, untimed, and failure-free with its live polygon signal-drift/signal-lock oscilloscope (`cinematic.rs`); persist only daemon-validated completion, not local band positions.
- Do not add command-entry gameplay.
- Progressively unfold workspace tabs as systems unlock (Planet, Archive, System at start; Overview and Probes once the first scout deploys or stores/surveyed regions appear; Research only once research is actually unlocked; System Map only at solar horizon). Support a compact side-by-side Planet/Probes/Archive/Local layout down to `80×24`, scrollable Help modal on compact viewports, a `38×5` companion Pilot Strip (with compact session menu on `Q`), a `NEXT:` countdown badge in workspace footers, and opt-in `--title` (OSC 0) and `--bell` (BEL) notifications.
- Keep Planet as the default workspace and sole regional command surface. Pair the O(1)-indexed, surface-cached globe (including organic atmospheric contours, network links, pulsing campaign lead beacon, 1.2s gold knowledge-change radar flashes, and `[N]` jump-to-lead shortcut) with a persistent selected-region inspector that displays a polygon terrain vista (`vista.rs`) and puts complete local operations first: show identifiers, phase, live mission telemetry strips, interpolated progress for non-pausing timers, remaining time, published risk, capacity, local construction or core processing, local survey-queue/waypoint and extraction-route state, latest local report, discovered facts, truthful presence and mission relationships, contextual ready actions, and concise relevant blockers (hiding irrelevant rig/extract/relay rows on unknown regions).
- Keep spatial local navigation and actions inside Planet: `V` replaces the globe with the selected region's daemon-published 4-by-4 field (2-row pixel-art terrain silhouettes, animated fog/survey sweeps, compact base sprites, and module strips in `art.rs`, plus grounded outpost cutaways in the inspector) once that region has completed its regional survey; arrows move by row and column with wrapping, `N` focuses an actionable local lead sector, `Esc` or `V` returns to the globe, and the parent region remains explicit. A rejected `V` input must publish a visible notice explaining the regional-survey requirement when the selected region has no published local field, and keep the same blocker persistent in the selected-region action list. Distinguish uncharted, detected frontier, surveyed, occupied, and active sectors through text or symbols as well as color. Keep focused survey, local extraction, foothold, module construction and power-mode toggles, maintenance, visible project capacity, and accepted depot support in the local inspector (explaining the foundation gate for foothold/local extraction before `alpha_complete`). Let `G` cycle the balanced, cargo, and shielded extraction rigs only while the extractor is ready (and on Planet only on Core/deposit/extractor regions; on Probes auto-select Extractor before cycling); show the configured rig before commitment and the accepted rig, capacity, risk, and timing during a mission. Distinguish online, suspended, and enabled-but-starved modules. Do not create a second command model or duplicate local commands in Overview.
- Keep **Probes** (`probes.rs`) as a live polygon schematic inspection workspace built purely from authoritative `PlanetSnapshot` fields: show morphing Survey Scout and Extraction Probe blueprints (`cinematic.rs`), live `[G]` rig swapping when ready, animated assembly-gantry progress while rebuilding, rig comparison stats, active research/profile chips, `[Enter]` jump-to-region on Planet, and three clearly marked reserved hardpoint slots for future modular probe work.
- Never let text clip silently. Pre-wrap label/value panels with `textwrap::hang_wrap` (continuation rows align under the value column), render inspectors through `render_inspector_text` so the title shows `[PgDn ▼]`/`[PgUp ▲]` overflow cues, keep the Research selection visible with `selection_scroll`, split authored observation text on line breaks (`paragraph_lines`), size the Return Summary from wrapped rows with the acknowledge hint always visible, and truncate header/footer text only at word boundaries (`truncate_chars`; ASCII ellipsis wherever the ASCII tier may render). Keep local tiles equal-sized so spare rows become margin. Normalize letter keys to lowercase in `handle_key` so Shift and Caps Lock never block commands. Behind modals, `dim_backdrop` treats `-`/`=` as borders only in the ASCII tier and only when they continue a rule.
- Keep mapped coverage, gathered and refined stores, fabrication, active profile, and analysis visible on Planet even while the inspector scrolls. Keep Overview deeper, aggregate, and non-commanding: show per-material stores and requirements, processing, fleet, network, active operations with risk and capacity, construction, and reports with hanging-indent wrapping; let selection return to the associated Planet region without dispatching gameplay. Keep Research as the direction, progress, completion, and profile-selection surface with its half-block topology diagram (`cinematic::build_research_topology_buffer`). Keep Archive as the permanent player-visible record of discovered observations, visually separating observation from current interpretation with per-record measurement diagrams (`transmission.rs`) and full-screen `[V]` plate replay; show only the daemon-published archive experiment with its live twin-trace waveform (`art.rs`), its progress or visible blocker, and use Enter to start it (never client-interpolate the pausable archive experiment timer). Present unacknowledged archive records as full-screen Discovery Transmissions (`transmission.rs`) with a 350 ms carrier-acquire lock, polygon plates, pre-wrapped typewriter reveal, and `Up`/`Down`/`PgUp`/`PgDn` scrolling on narrow viewports (`Space`/`Enter` reveals then acknowledges via `mind.acknowledge_archive_presentation`), and toggle the `[C]` Chapter Briefing overlay on demand. Keep System as the retained telemetry, frame-time, and diagnostics view. Hide System Map until the daemon-owned solar-horizon milestone and final approved observation, then open it automatically with an animated client-local parallax pullback (anchoring `CURRENT WORLD` dynamically outside the world sprite across the full pullback) and in-fiction `SOLAR HORIZON` closing card that identifies only the local star, current world, and intermittent unresolved outer returns.
- Display the daemon-owned campaign header (`CH I..V`, step pips, foreground question, and `NEXT //` strip on Planet) and objective in every gameplay workspace. Show exact published risk tiers, and confirm risky extraction and material-consuming construction inside the inspector while leaving the globe visible.
- Let arrows select adjacent regions on Planet, Shift-arrows jump across the 10-by-20 grid, `N` jump to the campaign lead region (or local lead sector), `C` toggle the Chapter Briefing card, arrows select entries on Overview, Research, Archive, and the session menu, Page Up and Page Down reveal deep Planet and Overview detail, Enter invoke the contextual primary action or selected entry, Esc cancel confirmations or return, `Q` open the session menu, and Tab move between workspaces; keep frequent actions visible in context.
- Auto-acknowledge live-watched activity on draw/detach so initial reattachment only presents away-time unreviewed player-visible operations, quotes newly recorded unpresented archive evidence verbatim, shows current stores, current objective, and two or three useful workspace-level next actions. Never stack Session Menu over Return Summary. Acknowledge through the typed daemon operation without discarding unacknowledged discovery transmissions, and never infer hidden progress or interpretation in the client.
- Require clear confirmation for destructive actions and display automation scope, approvals, and audit results when those systems exist.

## Rendering and validation

- Use the centralized Substrate Dark theme (`Theme::for_color_tier`) and universal 5-tier half-block polygon rasterizer (`raster/`) instead of widget-local color literals or specialized image protocols (never emit Kitty, Sixel, or iTerm2 graphics escapes). Keep its Solarized- and EGA-derived deep canvas, layered panel, raised, and cobalt blueprint surfaces, warm text hierarchy, cyan/gold/amber/magenta semantic roles, full-frame xterm-256 and 16-color indexed ANSI fallbacks (zero `Color::Rgb` cells across the entire rendered frame in `ansi256` and `ansi16` modes), and ≥4.5:1 contrast tests. Keep focused panels in double-line borders (`border::DOUBLE` / `ASCII_DOUBLE_BORDER`).
- Prefer graphical and spatial presentation when it communicates the world or an operation better than prose alone. Draw on late-1980s and early-1990s pixel-art principles through strong silhouettes, limited palettes, tile-like composition, terminal-native local scenes, compact sprites, diagrams, and expressive but economical animation; do not imitate a specific game or platform.
- Make graphics operationally meaningful: use them to convey hierarchy, position, movement, risk, progress, and change, and keep the same actionable information explicit in ASCII, monochrome, narrow, and low-capability modes.
- Reserve bright borders for the active surface and amber borders for modal or warning surfaces. Keep inactive borders and secondary text quiet; express every state through text, symbols, borders, or modifiers as well as color.
- Fill the full terminal and panel surfaces in color mode. Never force foreground or background colors in `--no-color`, `NO_COLOR`, or `TERM=dumb` mode; preserve hierarchy there with ASCII-safe symbols and modifiers.
- Render the regional world through a true orthographic near-hemisphere projection. Use half-block foreground/background colors for square Unicode subpixels, continuous lighting and limb depth, subtle projected region boundaries, and overlays for selection, scout position, and active paths. Never sample undiscovered world truth to add visual texture.
- Keep yaw, pitch, idle rotation, easing, and animation phase transient and client-local. Begin globe rotation after 30 seconds without keyboard input, stop it on the next input, pause it outside Planet and during help or confirmation, and never change selection or advance authoritative missions.
- Preserve side-by-side wide layouts, stacked narrow layouts, a useful tiny-terminal message, Unicode defaults, and equivalent `--ascii`, `--no-color`, `TERM=dumb`, and `NO_COLOR` behavior.
- Test palette semantics and essential contrast, then exercise reducers and Ratatui `TestBackend` frames for the startup/session menu, destructive new-playthrough confirmation and rollback, graceful-stop waiting state, wide, medium-footer, persistent stores, complete regional and local operation details, the 4-by-4 field and its fog/frontier/observed/occupied states, parent context, coordinate navigation, power, base connectivity, integrity, projects, automation, return summaries, Research, permanent Archive observation/interpretation separation and experiment control, the final System Map in Unicode/color and ASCII/monochrome, stacked and scrolled inspectors, tiny, disconnected, stale, help, globe and local selection, adjacent and fast regional navigation, the 30-second idle threshold and interruption, workspace navigation, deep Overview details, local presence, scout deployment and recall, regional and local extraction, rebuilding, refining, relay and base construction, maintenance, objectives, contextual actions, and confirmations.
- Finish with live PTY inspection against the real daemon. Trigger a diagnostic pulse, force disconnect/reconnect, verify detach leaves the daemon reachable, verify new playthrough returns to fresh onboarding, and verify Save & Stop restores the terminal and makes the daemon unreachable.
