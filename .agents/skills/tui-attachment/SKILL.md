# TUI attachment

## Permanent boundaries

- Attach to an existing daemon; never spawn, kill, or simulate authoritative game state. Submit graceful shutdown and new-playthrough requests only through their explicit typed daemon operations.
- Render authoritative snapshots and send typed actions through one connection worker responsible for handshake, subscriptions, requests, unsubscribe, and reconnect.
- Limit local state to view focus, selections, onboarding alignment proposals, contextual UI state, bounded received caches, observed delivery rate, scroll state, help, and stale age.
- Never read daemon logs or reconstruct hidden resource, mission, world, research, or story values in widgets.
- Retry failures every second, retain the latest snapshot with an obvious stale indicator, make immediate retry available, and distinguish detach-without-shutdown from the session menu's explicit save-and-stop request.
- Render from pure application state and restore raw mode, cursor, and screen on every exit, error, and panic path.

## Current and target interfaces

- Open a session menu on launch and with `Q`. Keep Continue, Detach, Save & Stop, and New Playthrough semantically distinct: Detach leaves simulation active; Save & Stop waits for daemon acknowledgement before restoring the terminal; New Playthrough requires a second confirmation and reports persistence rollback clearly. Never delete or rewrite save files in the TUI.
- Keep the Stage 0 Coherence onboarding graphical, untimed, and failure-free with its live signal-drift/signal-lock oscilloscope (`art.rs`); persist only daemon-validated completion, not local band positions.
- Do not add command-entry gameplay.
- Keep Planet as the default workspace and sole regional command surface. Pair the globe (including its pulsing campaign lead beacon and `[N]` jump-to-lead shortcut) with a persistent selected-region inspector that puts complete local operations first: show identifiers, phase, live mission telemetry strips, progress, remaining time, published risk, capacity, local construction or core processing, local survey-queue and extraction-route state, latest local report, discovered facts, truthful presence and mission relationships, every regional action, and an individual reason for each unavailable action.
- Keep spatial local navigation and actions inside Planet: `V` replaces the globe with the selected region's daemon-published 4-by-4 field (2-row pixel-art terrain silhouettes, animated fog/survey sweeps, compact base sprites, and module strips in `art.rs`) once that region has completed its regional survey; arrows move by row and column with wrapping, `Esc` or `V` returns to the globe, and the parent region remains explicit. A rejected `V` input must publish a visible notice explaining the regional-survey requirement when the selected region has no published local field, and keep the same blocker persistent in the selected-region action list. Distinguish uncharted, detected frontier, surveyed, occupied, and active sectors through text or symbols as well as color. Keep focused survey, local extraction, foothold, module construction and power-mode toggles, maintenance, visible project capacity, and accepted depot support in the local inspector (explaining the foundation gate for foothold/local extraction before `alpha_complete`). Let `G` cycle the balanced, cargo, and shielded extraction rigs only while the extractor is ready; show the configured rig before commitment and the accepted rig, capacity, risk, and timing during a mission. Distinguish online, suspended, and enabled-but-starved modules. Do not create a second command model or duplicate local commands in Overview.
- Keep mapped coverage, gathered and refined stores, fabrication, active profile, and analysis visible on Planet even while the inspector scrolls. Keep Overview deeper, aggregate, and non-commanding: show per-material stores and requirements, processing, fleet, network, active operations with risk and capacity, construction, and reports; let selection return to the associated Planet region without dispatching gameplay. Keep Research as the direction, progress, completion, and profile-selection surface. Keep Archive as the permanent player-visible record of discovered observations, visually separating observation from current interpretation with per-record measurement diagrams (`transmission.rs`); show only the daemon-published archive experiment with its live twin-trace waveform (`art.rs`), its progress or visible blocker, and use Enter to start it. Present unacknowledged archive records as full-screen Discovery Transmissions (`transmission.rs`) with a typewriter reveal (`Space`/`Enter` reveals then acknowledges via `mind.acknowledge_archive_presentation`), and toggle the `[C]` Chapter Briefing overlay on demand. Keep System as the retained telemetry and diagnostics view. Hide System Map until the daemon-owned solar-horizon milestone and final approved observation, then open it automatically with an animated client-local parallax pullback and in-fiction `SOLAR HORIZON` closing card that identifies only the local star, current world, and intermittent unresolved outer returns.
- Display the daemon-owned campaign header (`CH I..V`, step pips, foreground question, and `NEXT //` strip on Planet) and objective in every gameplay workspace. Show exact published risk tiers, and confirm risky extraction and material-consuming construction inside the inspector while leaving the globe visible.
- Let arrows select adjacent regions on Planet, Shift-arrows jump across the 10-by-20 grid, `N` jump to the campaign lead region, `C` toggle the Chapter Briefing card, arrows select entries on Overview, Research, Archive, and the session menu, Page Up and Page Down reveal deep Planet and Overview detail, Enter invoke the contextual primary action or selected entry, Esc cancel confirmations or return, `Q` open the session menu, and Tab move between workspaces; keep frequent actions visible in context.
- On initial reattachment, show unreviewed player-visible operations, quote newly recorded archive evidence verbatim, show current stores, current objective, and two or three useful workspace-level next actions. Acknowledge through the typed daemon operation without discarding unacknowledged discovery transmissions, and never infer hidden progress or interpretation in the client.
- Require clear confirmation for destructive actions and display automation scope, approvals, and audit results when those systems exist.

## Rendering and validation

- Use the centralized Substrate Dark theme instead of widget-local color literals. Keep its Solarized-derived deep canvas, layered panel and raised surfaces, warm text hierarchy, restrained cyan focus and communication, amber actions, and distinct success, warning, and failure roles.
- Prefer graphical and spatial presentation when it communicates the world or an operation better than prose alone. Draw on late-1980s and early-1990s pixel-art principles through strong silhouettes, limited palettes, tile-like composition, terminal-native local scenes, compact sprites, diagrams, and expressive but economical animation; do not imitate a specific game or platform.
- Make graphics operationally meaningful: use them to convey hierarchy, position, movement, risk, progress, and change, and keep the same actionable information explicit in ASCII, monochrome, narrow, and low-capability modes.
- Reserve bright borders for the active surface and amber borders for modal or warning surfaces. Keep inactive borders and secondary text quiet; express every state through text, symbols, borders, or modifiers as well as color.
- Fill the full terminal and panel surfaces in color mode. Never force foreground or background colors in `--no-color`, `NO_COLOR`, or `TERM=dumb` mode; preserve hierarchy there with ASCII-safe symbols and modifiers.
- Render the regional world through a true orthographic near-hemisphere projection. Use half-block foreground/background colors for square Unicode subpixels, continuous lighting and limb depth, subtle projected region boundaries, and overlays for selection, scout position, and active paths. Never sample undiscovered world truth to add visual texture.
- Keep yaw, pitch, idle rotation, easing, and animation phase transient and client-local. Begin globe rotation after 30 seconds without keyboard input, stop it on the next input, pause it outside Planet and during help or confirmation, and never change selection or advance authoritative missions.
- Preserve side-by-side wide layouts, stacked narrow layouts, a useful tiny-terminal message, Unicode defaults, and equivalent `--ascii`, `--no-color`, `TERM=dumb`, and `NO_COLOR` behavior.
- Test palette semantics and essential contrast, then exercise reducers and Ratatui `TestBackend` frames for the startup/session menu, destructive new-playthrough confirmation and rollback, graceful-stop waiting state, wide, medium-footer, persistent stores, complete regional and local operation details, the 4-by-4 field and its fog/frontier/observed/occupied states, parent context, coordinate navigation, power, base connectivity, integrity, projects, automation, return summaries, Research, permanent Archive observation/interpretation separation and experiment control, the final System Map in Unicode/color and ASCII/monochrome, stacked and scrolled inspectors, tiny, disconnected, stale, help, globe and local selection, adjacent and fast regional navigation, the 30-second idle threshold and interruption, workspace navigation, deep Overview details, local presence, scout deployment and recall, regional and local extraction, rebuilding, refining, relay and base construction, maintenance, objectives, contextual actions, and confirmations.
- Finish with live PTY inspection against the real daemon. Trigger a diagnostic pulse, force disconnect/reconnect, verify detach leaves the daemon reachable, verify new playthrough returns to fresh onboarding, and verify Save & Stop restores the terminal and makes the daemon unreachable.
